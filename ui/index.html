How to play

  1. Choose Play above to open Minecraft Launcher and select the managed profile.
  2. In Minecraft Launcher, select Mario 64 in Minecraft and press Play.
  3. Enter a single-player world. Press M with chat closed to switch into Mario.

If the official launcher changes the selected installation, choose “Mario 64 in Minecraft” again from its bottom-left dropdown.
